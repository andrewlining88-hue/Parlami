01

Chat tab

This is home base — your ongoing conversation with Dante.

👋

Every time you log in

Dante greets you with a menu. Reply with a number, or just type what you want in your own words.

1
🎭
Role playRestaurant, shopping, travel, doctor, airport — Dante sets the scene and stays in character.
2
🔄
Verb practiceConversational, not a drill — Dante mixes tenses naturally into chat.
3
💬
Free chatTalk about anything. Dante suggests topics if you're stuck.
4
📝
Grammar drillsOne concept at a time, taught through real conversation.
💡Want to switch activity mid-chat? Send the number again — Dante drops whatever you were doing and jumps straight to the new one.
🧠

How Dante talks to you

🇮🇹

Adapts to your level

A1 gets Italian-first with English translations. By B1+, it's almost entirely in Italian.

🔊

Listen to anything

Tap the speaker icon on any message to hear it read aloud.

✏️

Gentle corrections

Dante fixes your biggest mistake, briefly, and moves on — never a wall of red ink.

📚

Knows your lessons

If your teacher has added notes, Dante references them and any new vocabulary from your last lesson.

02

Exercises tab

Structured practice, separate from free conversation.

✅

Practice exercises

A fresh set of 6 exercises every time — 3 fill-in-the-blank, 3 multiple choice — generated for your exact level. If your teacher has added a lesson note or new vocabulary, the exercises are built around that. Dante also targets your recurring mistakes on purpose, so weak spots get extra reps.

🃏

Smart flashcards

Flip through your saved vocabulary. Mark each word "got it" or "don't know it" — the deck remembers. Words you struggle with come back sooner; words you know well fade out. Every session is built from what you're actually about to forget, not a random shuffle.

03

Vocab tab

Every word you encounter, organized automatically.

⭐

Today's Words

New words from today's chat, collected automatically. They move into your main vocab list the next day.

🗂️

Auto-categorized

Dante sorts every word into groups — Verbi, Cibo, Viaggio, Espressioni, and more — with English translation and pronunciation.

🔤

Three display modes

Switch between Italian only, Italian + English, or Italian + English + phonetic pronunciation.

✅

Star or master a word

⭐ words you want to focus on, ✅ mark words you've mastered — they move to a separate "Mastered" list.

➕You can also add your own words directly — anything you look up outside a chat, so nothing gets lost.

Add Parlami to your home screen

Get one-tap access, just like a real app — no App Store needed.

🍎

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1
Open parlami.chat in Safari (this must be Safari, not Chrome).
2
Tap the Share icon ⬆ at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
4
Tap "Add" in the top corner. Parlami now appears as an icon on your home screen.
🤖

Android (Chrome)

1
Open parlami.chat in Chrome.
2
Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
3
Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app."
4
Confirm by tapping "Add." Parlami now opens full-screen, just like a native app.
⚡Once installed, Parlami opens instantly without the browser address bar — and works even with a spotty connection.
